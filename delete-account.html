Delete Your ZeroZero Account

If you would like us to permanently delete your ZeroZero account and associated data, please send us an email.

  1. Create a new email.
  2. Send it to support.zerozero@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject: Delete My ZeroZero Account.
  4. Include the email address used to sign in to ZeroZero.
  5. If available, include your display name.
  6. We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and associated data.
